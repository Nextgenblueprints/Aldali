About Us

For over 20 years, we've been in the business of

Empowering Kuwait's Economic Growth

Founded in 2003 as a private company offering Heavy Equipment and Machinery support services to the industries in Kuwait, today DIC has exponentially grown and established itself as one of the most cherished multi-diversified contractors across the State of Kuwait. DIC's exponential growth since its modest beginning is a reflection of its commitment to customers, who come year after year.

DIC with dedicated and experienced Human assets of 800+ and Equipment assets exceeding 1000+ Heavy Equipment & Machinery, along with innovative technological partners, is marching towards achieving its vision of becoming a market leader and preferred partner for Integrated Energy and Infrastructure sectors.
