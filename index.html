About Us

Welcome to
Al Dali International

Co. for General Trading & Contracting W.L.L

Founded in 2003 as a private Company offering Heavy Equipment and Machinery support services to the Industries in Kuwait, today DIC has exponentially grown and established itself as one of the most cherished multi diversified contractor across the State of Kuwait. DIC exponential growth since its modest beginning is a reflection of its commitment to customers, who comes year after year.

DIC with dedicated and experienced Human assets of 800+ nos. and with Equipment assets exceeding 1000+ nos. of Heavy Equipment & Machinery along with Innovative technological partners, is marching towards achieving its vision of becoming a market leader and preferred partner for Integrated Energy and Infrastructure sectors services provider closer to the heart of its clients.
