About Us

Over 20 years of integrated energy & infrastructure excellence across Kuwait.

Our Story

From Humble Beginnings to Market Leadership

Founded in 2003 as a private Company offering Heavy Equipment and Machinery support services to the industries in Kuwait, today DIC has exponentially grown and established itself as one of the most cherished multi-diversified contractors across the State of Kuwait.

DIC's exponential growth since its modest beginning is a reflection of its commitment to customers, who come year after year.
