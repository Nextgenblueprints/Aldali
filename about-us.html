Rising to Excellence

DIC's remarkable journey

Founded in 2003 as a private company offering heavy equipment and machinery support services to industries in Kuwait, DIC has grown into a multi-diversified contractor across the State of Kuwait.

With 800+ experienced human assets and 1000+ heavy equipment and machinery assets, DIC is marching toward its vision of becoming a market leader and preferred partner for integrated energy and infrastructure services.

QHSE

Quality, Health, Safety & Environment

QHSE stewardship is ingrained in DIC operations and service delivery. Management, employees, and contractors are responsible for driving a strong QHSE culture through standards, reporting, training, and continuous improvement.

  • Clear QHSE objectives and measurable targets.
  • Environmental risk management and climate-related mitigation.
  • Strong reporting culture, near-miss investigation, and accident prevention.
  • Workforce training, stakeholder communication, and emergency response planning.
